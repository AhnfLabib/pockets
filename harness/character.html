Pockets

One whole visit as the extension will play it: Pockets pokes his head in from a random edge, maybe says something, climbs the nearer border, crosses under the tab he wants, warns with a countdown card, takes the tab and leaves by a random edge. Every Play picks a new seed (shown under the buttons), so no two visits look the same. Nothing on this page is a copy; it runs the extension's own modules.

Try Keep yourself

Press “Play it” and click “Keep this tab” on the card during the countdown. Pockets says “Fine. Keep it.” and leaves empty-pawed. The second button presses Keep for you after two seconds.

The tab strip

While the card counts down, the target tab's icon becomes Pockets and its title counts down too, as the extension will do on the real tab. After the theft the notice offers Undo.

Reduced motion

With “reduce motion” on in the system settings there is no crawl: the card appears at the top left and Pockets sits still beside it.
