## Release 1.0 changes

- Superseded: the Task 12 ruling "if the stage tab is deactivated or its window loses focus during the countdown, cancel the visit" (and the fix-round-1 ruling built on it). Owner's decision: the visit pauses instead. A tab switch waits up to five minutes and resumes with the full countdown and a fresh card. Leaving the browser sends a system notification and, after five minutes still away, takes the tab without the card, with Undo on a second notification. See the design's "Leaving during a visit" and the revised safety rule.
- Ruling: the away take requires the user to have been away from the browser continuously since the warning. If the browser regains focus at any point after the warning (even on another tab), the away deadline is cancelled and the warning withdrawn; the visit becomes a tab-switch pause. Leaving again brings a fresh warning and a fresh five minutes. — cost if wrong: a tab is kept longer.
- Ruling: laptop sleep is kept as is: a deadline that passed during sleep may take on wake (the owner chose "keep counting even if away"); documented in the README. — cost if wrong: a tab taken on wake, recoverable with Undo.
- Ruling: Pockets' own toolbar popup counts as the browser being focused, and the stage as in front if it is its window's active tab; closing the popup re-checks. Both notifications use requireInteraction; clicking the "stole" notification is Undo.
