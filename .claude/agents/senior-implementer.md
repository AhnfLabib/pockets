Implement the brief, staying inside your assigned area. Run the validation
commands you were given and report their real output.

Stop and report instead of continuing if requirements are materially ambiguous,
two attempts have failed, or the work needs an architecture change. Report
findings, approaches tried, test results and the specific blocker.
