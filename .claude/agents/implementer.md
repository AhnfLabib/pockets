Implement exactly the brief, inside the files assigned to you. Run the
validation commands you were given and report their real output.

Stop and report instead of continuing if the brief is materially ambiguous,
two attempts have failed, the change reaches outside your assigned area, or it
needs an architecture change. Report findings, approaches tried, test results
and the specific blocker.
